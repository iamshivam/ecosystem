- **Hyperscaler capex is now a macro-scale number.** Goldman Sachs pegs the four big cloud companies (Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta) at $725B in combined capex for 2026 — up 77% year-on-year. Miten Sampat steps in as interim CEO. The transaction is simultaneously India's largest fintech funding event and its most prominent founder exit. (TechCrunch)

## 8. Banks & Regulation — RBI & Global

- **RBI (India): repo rate held at 5.25%, stance neutral.** The MPC vote was unanimous. GDP growth forecast cut to 6.6% (from 6.9%); inflation forecast raised to 5.1% (from 4.6%). RBI also expanded the Fully Accessible Route (FAR) for government securities and eased norms for overseas investors — signalling a focus on foreign inflow attraction even as domestic conditions stay cautious. (Forbes India)

- **SEBI board (June 19) approved a clutch of capital market reforms:** Share buybacks through open-market exchanges reinstated effective August 1 (reversing a 2024 restriction); mutual funds permitted intraday borrowing for operational needs; securitisation governance aligned with RBI frameworks. (JSA Law)

- **SEBI chairman flagged inter-regulator gridlock on commodity derivatives:** RBI and IRDAI are both resisting allowing banks and insurance companies into commodity derivatives markets. Tuhin Kanta Pandey called publicly for "smart regulation" to break the standoff — a rare public expression of frustration from a regulator toward peer regulators. (Moneylife)

- **US Federal Reserve (June 17): rate held at 3.50–3.75%, zero 2026 cuts now projected.** The dot plot eliminated any 2026 rate cut; nine of 18 members now favour a hike before year-end. Inflation rising on energy price pressure from the Iran conflict. Next catalyst: July FOMC meeting. (CNBC, Federal Reserve)

- **ECB (June 11): raised all three key rates by 25 bps** — deposit facility to 2.25%, main refinancing to 2.40%, marginal lending to 2.65%, effective June 17. Driven by Middle East war-linked energy inflation; eurozone inflation now projected at 3.0% for 2026. (ECB)

- **US Basel III / Capital Rules:** Banking agencies proposed reducing regulatory capital requirements for large internationally active banks, replacing the dual-stack calculation with a single risk-sensitive method. Community Bank Leverage Ratio cut from 9% to 8%. Public comment period closed June 18. (Holland & Knight)

- **GENIUS Act (US stablecoin):** Federal banking agencies face a July 18 deadline to adopt a comprehensive stablecoin regulatory framework. Among the most consequential near-term fintech policy events globally. (Paul Hastings)

- **Bank of England fined Vocalink £11.9M** for compliance failures (reduced from £20M for early cooperation). UK legislation bringing crypto custody, issuance, and trading venues into the regulated perimeter expected to finalise in 2026. (Bank of England)
